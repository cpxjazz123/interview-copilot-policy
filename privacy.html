Interview Copilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Interview Copilot is a Chrome extension that transcribes interview audio you explicitly capture, extracts the interviewer's questions, and optionally forwards them to an agent endpoint that you configure. It has no developer-operated backend: nothing is sent to the extension's author.

What the extension handles

DataWhenWhere it goes
Audio from the tab or microphone you start on Only between pressing Start and Stop Streamed to Deepgram for transcription with the API key you enter in Options. Never stored by the extension.
Transcript text While recording Kept in memory; written to <Downloads>/file/transcript.txt (path configurable).
Extracted questions While recording or on Extract now Kept in memory; written to <Downloads>/file/question.txt. Optionally sent to CommandCode for LLM extraction with your own key.
Settings (API keys, endpoints, file paths) When you save Options chrome.storage.local on this device.

Optional delivery

If you enable delivery in Options, the extension POSTs newly detected questions to the HTTP endpoint you provide (for example your own agent service). That endpoint is chosen by you; the extension ships with no endpoint configured and sends nothing while delivery mode is none.

Accounts on this site

The extension has no account system, but this website does. When you create an account on interview.cpxjazzx.com we store the following in a Cloudflare D1 database (region OC) in our Cloudflare account:

DataWhy
Your email address To sign you in, to email you a confirmation or password-reset link, and to keep abuse down.
Your name, if you type it or if Google supplies it Shown on your account page.
When you sign in with Google: Google's account identifier (sub) and your profile picture URL To link the Google account to the right Interview Copilot account. We never see your Google password.
When you use a password: a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash of it The password itself is never stored, and the hash cannot be turned back into a password.
Session tokens (stored as SHA-256 hashes), the client IP address and the browser user-agent To keep you signed in and to detect automated abuse.
One-time email-confirmation and password-reset tokens (stored as hashes) They expire after 24 hours and 1 hour respectively, and work once.
Rate-limit counters whose key contains your email address or IP address To slow down repeated sign-up, sign-in and email requests. They expire after an hour.
Timestamps: when the account was created and when it last signed in Account history.

None of it is sold, shared with advertisers, or handed to anyone else. There is no analytics or advertising SDK on this site either.

How long it is kept

  • Sessions: 30 days, then they stop working.
  • Confirmation tokens: 24 hours. Reset tokens: 1 hour. Both single-use.
  • Rate-limit counters: 1 hour.
  • Your account: until you delete it.

Deleting your account

The Delete account button on your account page removes your account, every session and every pending token immediately. Password accounts have to type their password first. Ask us at the address below and we will delete it for you too.

Third parties

  • Deepgram (api.deepgram.com) — speech to text, using your account.
  • CommandCode (api.commandcode.ai) — optional LLM question extraction, using your account.
  • Your own endpoint — optional delivery of questions.

Each of these services processes data under its own terms. There is no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDK in the extension.

Retention and deletion

Transcript and question files stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them. In-memory state is cleared when you press Reset or when the service worker unloads. Removing the extension deletes its stored settings.

Permissions

  • tabCapture / activeTab — capture the audio of the tab you start recording on.
  • offscreen — keep the audio pipeline and websocket alive outside the popup.
  • storage — save your settings locally.
  • downloads / downloads.ui — write the transcript and question files.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information by itself.
